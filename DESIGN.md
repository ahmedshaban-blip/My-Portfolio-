# Design

Visual system design documentation for Ahmed Shaban's portfolio, outlining color strategy, custom typography, spacing, and interactive layout components.

---

## 🎨 Color Strategy: Committed Emerald Luxury

We completely reject generic AI-generated dark mode templates (typically `#020617` blue-slate with saturated purple/neon-blue glows). Instead, we use green-tinted neutrals and a deep-emerald primary with warm amber/bronze accents to invoke a feeling of meticulous craft.

### 🌗 Theme Mappings

#### Light Mode (Soft & Crisp)
- **Background:** Soft green-tinted neutral (`150 15% 95%`)
- **Primary:** Deep emerald (`162 70% 32%`)
- **Accent:** Warm bronze (`32 85% 45%`)

#### Dark Mode (Forest & Amber)
- **Background:** Deepest green-tinted charcoal (`160 12% 4%`)
- **Primary:** Luminous emerald (`160 60% 45%`)
- **Accent:** Rich amber (`36 85% 58%`)
- **Card Panels:** Green-tinted charcoal (`160 10% 6%`)

---

## ✍️ Typography

- **Headings & Main Labels:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (rich tracking-tight bold sans)
- **Code & Snippets:** `JetBrains Mono` or `Fira Code` (technical monospaced precision)
- **Body Text:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Inter` (high-readability, fluid-scaled, max 70ch line-width)

---

## 💫 Interactive Motion

- **Entrance Animations:** Staggered spring-motion reveals.
- **Micro-interactions:** Magnetic hover states on social links and primary action buttons.
- **Canvas Particle Flow:** Slow-fps background rendering of code snippets with matching theme-mode color gradients.
