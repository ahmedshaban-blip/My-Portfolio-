# Design

Visual system design documentation for Ahmed Shaban's portfolio, outlining color strategy, custom typography, spacing, and interactive layout components.

---

## 🎨 Color Strategy: Committed Warm Luxury

We completely reject generic AI-generated dark mode templates (typically `#020617` blue-slate with saturated purple/neon-blue glows). Instead, we use custom-tinted neutrals and metallic-luxe primary/accent tones to invoke a feeling of meticulous craft.

### 🌗 Theme Mappings

#### Light Mode (Soft & Crisp)
- **Background:** Soft blue-tinted cool gray (`210 20% 96%`)
- **Primary:** Warm royal blue (`221 83% 53%`)
- **Accent:** Royal purple (`262 83% 58%`)

#### Dark Mode (Obsidian & Liquid Amber)
- **Background:** Deepest carbon obsidian (`240 6% 3.5%`)
- **Primary:** Luxury liquid gold / champagne (`38 85% 62%`)
- **Accent:** Rich terracotta copper (`24 80% 55%`)
- **Card Panels:** Muted rose-tinted charcoal (`240 6% 5.5%`)

---

## ✍️ Typography

- **Headings & Main Labels:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (rich tracking-tight bold sans)
- **Code & Snippets:** `JetBrains Mono` or `Fira Code` (technical monospaced precision)
- **Body Text:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Inter` (high-readability, fluid-scaled, max 70ch line-width)

---

## 💫 Interactive Motion

- **Entrance Animations:** Staggered spring-motion reveals.
- **Micro-interactions:** Magnetic hover states on social links and primary action buttons.
- **Canvas Particle Flow:** Slow-fps background rendering of code snippets with matching theme-mode color gradients.
