# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Recruiters, engineering managers, and clients looking for a highly skilled, professional mobile and frontend developer specializing in Flutter, React Native, and React. They are usually reviewing multiple portfolios and seek clarity, speed, performance, and proof of exceptional craft.

## Product Purpose
To showcase Ahmed Shaban's professional projects, technical skills, and experience with high-fidelity polish, clean engineering, and exceptional aesthetic details, converting visitors into career opportunities.

## Brand Personality
Precise, Bespoke, and High-Performance.

## Anti-references
- The "AI Slop" template (slate-blue background with neon blue/purple gradients, huge rounded-corner cards with simple icons).
- Standard flat layouts with zero pacing or dynamic feel.
- Purely dark or light templates without warm, custom tints.

## Design Principles
- **Aesthetic Precision:** Every element, margin, and shadow is crafted with exact intent. No placeholders or lazy spacing.
- **Dynamic Vitality:** The interface feels alive with micro-interactions, spring animations, and responsive depth.
- **Physical Authenticity:** Use colors and gradients that reflect real-world, high-end design objects rather than software templates.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG AA color contrast compliance.
- Support for `prefers-reduced-motion` to smoothly bypass intensive physics animations.
