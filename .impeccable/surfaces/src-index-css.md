> Visitor mode: **Persuade** — the visitor decides and acts; the action is hire or reach out.

THESIS: The portfolio stops borrowing a warm metal and speaks in wine instead. It refuses the category default it previously sat next to: a neutral template with a bright copper-orange accent doing all the talking. Here the accent is bordeaux, and the grounds carry the depth.

OWN-WORLD: Bordeaux (`oklch` hue 352–358) on tinted zinc. Light mode is a faint rose paper (`0.982 0.003 350`) with cool zinc ink; dark mode is a near-black violet-cast zinc (`0.16 0.008 310`) with bone text. Primary and accent are one hue family 3° apart, on purpose: primary carries structure, accent carries secondary emphasis. Cards are opaque tinted zinc, borders are quiet, nothing is a pure gray.

STORY: A recruiter should read "considered and expensive" before reading a single project name, and never think "template". They should trust the craft in the first screen, then move to the work.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Unchanged composition — portrait plate centred, a two-line display headline under it, a live status chip above it, a quiet badge row below. Only the material moves: the ambient mesh, the name lockup, the chip, the tagline emphasis, and the browser surfaces all read bordeaux off the live tokens.
